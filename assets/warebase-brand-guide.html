Usage

Do

  • Keep clear space around the icon equal to at least the height of one bar
  • Use amber sparingly, as an accent on the tallest bar or key CTAs
  • Set the mono typeface only for data, codes, or labels

Don't

  • Recolor the icon outside the approved palette
  • Stretch or skew the wordmark
  • Place the light lockup on backgrounds lighter than Cool Mist
